Configure your Anthropic API key and preferred model.

Stored locally in your browser, never sent anywhere except api.anthropic.com. Get a key at console.anthropic.com/settings/keys.

Opus is the most capable. Sonnet is a good speed / cost balance. Haiku is fastest and cheapest.
